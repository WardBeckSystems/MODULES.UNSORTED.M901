## Power supply help

_WBS TECHNICAL SUPPORT · Dec 13, 2013|46_

> According to the spec sheets:
> M462 - 100 mA Max
> M466 - 100 Ma Max
> M490 - 80 Ma Max
> M901 - Not sure.
>
> So you're looking at 780 mA plus what-ever the 901's want...
>
> It's the exact same PSU design that I have available... 317/337 design with a voltage doubler to get the 48V.
>
> You may be able to squeeze enough power out of one of their kits... But I would install two supply cards if I were wiring everything up. (A larger transformer can feed more than 1 card!)
